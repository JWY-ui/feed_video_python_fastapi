# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young content consumers and creators on a short-video platform. Viewers scroll through feeds, like, comment, follow creators, and chat via DMs. Creators upload videos, manage their profile, and track follower/like counts. Mobile-first scrolling mindset: the feed IS the interface.

## Product Purpose

A short-video feed platform with full social features: likes, comments, follows, tags, trending ranking, DMs, and real-time notifications. The core experience is an immersive vertical video feed — content first, chrome minimal.

## Brand Personality

Young, efficient, anime/ACG aesthetic. Think Bilibili (B站): playful but not childish, energetic but not chaotic. The interface should feel alive — rounded corners, bold accent colors, subtle animations — without slowing anyone down. Efficiency matters: scroll, watch, react, move on. No friction.

- Reference: Bilibili — the way it balances dense content with playful visual identity; distinctive without being loud.
- Anti-reference: Dull enterprise dashboards. Over-commercialized feeds (too many badges, banners, upsells). Corporate seriousness.

## Design Principles

1. **Content-first, chrome-second.** The video feed is the hero. UI chrome is minimal and transparent during playback, surfacing only when the user pauses or interacts.
2. **Playful precision.** Rounded corners, bold accent colors, micro-animations on like/follow — but everything serves a function. Motion is quick and purposeful, never decorative.
3. **Speed as personality.** Scroll, react, move on. Transitions are fast. Buttons respond instantly. Loading states are brief and branded. Slowness kills the youthful energy.
4. **Density without clutter.** Feed cards pack author info, stats, and actions into a compact space — the B站 lesson: you can be information-rich and still feel light.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise/admin dashboard UIs: gray tables, form-heavy layouts, conservative typography
- Over-commercialized feeds: excessive badges, banners, upsells, "premium" markers
- Sterile minimalism: too much whitespace, too little personality

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basic readability. No specific WCAG target. Standard best-effort: sufficient contrast, legible type sizes, keyboard-navigable where practical.
